# 📚 Hi Neo — this is YOUR project!

This is a little website called **Book Swap**. It shows books that kids
want to trade with each other. Right now it has some made-up books in it.
Your job is to make it your own. 🎉

---

## ▶️ Step 1 — See it

Find the file called **index.html** in this folder and **double-click it**.
It will open in your web browser (like Chrome). That's your website! 🖥️

---

## ✍️ Step 2 — Add your own book

1. Open the file called **books.js** in your code editor.
   (Ask Dad to show you how to open it the first time.)
2. Read the steps written at the very top of that file.
3. Add a book you'd really swap.
4. **Save** the file (press `Ctrl` and `S` together).
5. Go back to your browser and **refresh** (press `F5`).

👀 Did your book pop up? You just changed a website. That's coding!

---

## 🎨 Step 3 — Change how it looks

Open **styles.css**. Near the top you'll see a list of colors.
Change one of them (like the orange) and refresh the page.
Keep going until it looks the way YOU like it.

---

---

## 😅 If something breaks

Don't worry — nothing is ruined, ever. Usually it's one of these:

- A missing **"quote"** around some words
- A missing **comma** `,` at the end of a line
- A missing **curly brace** `{` or `}`

Look at the other books — copy the way they're written. If you're stuck,
you can always undo with `Ctrl` + `Z`, or ask Dad.

---

## 💡 Remember

There is no "wrong" here. This is **your** experiment. Try stuff.
Break stuff. Fix stuff. That's exactly how coders learn. Have fun! 🚀
